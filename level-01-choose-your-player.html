Level One · Self-paced · Day 1

Welcome, Player One.

Who's logging in, and what are they trying to do?

Before you build a single thing in Qualtrics, you've got a choice to make: who's playing? An admin? A frontline manager? An exec who only logs in for the dashboard? Each one needs a different setup, and that setup gets harder to fix later. Better to make the call now.

Spend 20 minutes reading. Spend 20 minutes building your player cards. Bring those cards to your team — they're the start of every conversation about who gets access to what.

What you leave with

  • Two to four named players, each with a defined goal
  • A unique identifier strategy for both customers and employees
  • A printable player card set for internal alignment

Beat 01 / Read

Three players. Three setups.

Spend 20 minutes here. Each block ends with a question. Hold onto your answers — you'll bring them straight into the card builder below.

A

The Admin

The person who keeps the platform running. Sets up users, defines roles, manages the directory, owns the field naming conventions everyone else inherits. Touches every project, builds none of them end-to-end.

An admin needs broad access and a clear view of who's doing what. They're the difference between a clean platform in year two and a mess.

Carry into Beat 2 Who in your org will own the platform itself?
M

The Frontline Manager

The person who actually acts on the data. Sees their team's dashboard on Monday morning, sees a flagged ticket in the afternoon, follows up with a customer or an employee by Friday.

They need a focused view — their team, their region, their metrics. Anything more and the dashboard goes unread. The setup that works for the admin will sink the manager.

Carry into Beat 2 Which managers will live in Qualtrics weekly?
E

The Executive

The person who only logs in for the dashboard, and only for the top-line numbers. Wants the big picture in 60 seconds. Doesn't want to filter, drill down, or learn the tool.

An exec view is a different design problem from a manager view. Same data, ruthless edit. Get this wrong and they stop opening the link.

Carry into Beat 2 Which leaders will see the rollup view?

Beat 02 / Build

Build your player cards.

🔒 Finish Beat 1 to unlock the builder.

Two to four players. Real names if you have them, role names if you don't. Each card needs a goal — what is this player actually trying to do? — and a unique identifier. Cards save as you type.

The rules

Two minimums, four maximum. More than four and you're naming everyone in the org chart instead of designing for actual users.

Players defined 0 / 4
  • Name a person or a clear role — "Sarah from Ops" or "Regional CX Manager"
  • Goal is one sentence, ends in a verb the player does
  • One identifier strategy for customers, one for employees
  • Skip the maybes. Add only the players who'll log in
Beat 03 / Print

Print your party.

🔒 Define at least two players to unlock the export.

The card set you just built is the artifact you bring to your team. One page. Printable. Use it in the next stakeholder meeting — it's the start of every conversation about who gets access to what.

What you do with this

Nobody else has to fill this out for you. The work is already done. Print the page and move on.
