Module Three · Live webinar · Day 9

Live

Close the loop.

Qualtrics → Salesforce. 90 minutes. The integration runs both ways by the end.

Live Session 1 sent data one direction. Live Session 2 sends it back. You'll build an event-driven workflow that fires when a survey response comes in, evaluates the response with a decision step, and writes a field back to the original Salesforce record.

By the end, the loop runs both ways — Salesforce ships the request, Qualtrics handles the response, and Salesforce sees the update. You'll watch the field change on-screen.

Bring the test survey response from your side quest. If you completed the survey by accident, take it again — we need an unfinished response to demo against.

Rules of the room

Four things that make this work.

  1. 1 Object ID is the deliverable. Build 4 is the most consequential build of the session. If the Object ID isn't mapped, the write-back has nothing to find. We slow down there.
  2. 2 The decision step is editorial. Not every response should write back. The decision step turns the integration from a firehose into a signal. You'll set your own threshold.
  3. 3 Stage the verification beat. When the field updates on-screen in Salesforce, take the pause. It's the closing win of the entire lab — don't rush past it.
  4. 4 Workflows that ship are workflows that are active. A surprising number of customers leave workflows in draft. Build 5 is "save and arm" for a reason — confirm out loud.

The 90 minutes

What happens during the build.

Five builds, two verifications, one closing path-to-production conversation. The verification beat in Salesforce is the moment the lab snaps into focus.

0:00 open

Kickoff · Chat poll

Who completed the side quest? Who accidentally took the survey?

Both answers earn applause. Producer notes who needs a fresh distribution link in the first ten minutes.

⏱ 3 min
0:03 anchor

Context

Event-driven versus scheduled.

Live 1 was scheduled — workflow runs on a clock. Live 2 is event-driven — workflow runs on a trigger. Same architecture, opposite cadence.

⏱ 5 min
0:08 build 1

In your survey

Confirm the Object ID is in the survey flow.

Open the survey from Live 1 and review the embedded data block. The Salesforce Object ID must be present — it's the single most important field. If it's missing for anyone, we fix it before moving on.

⏱ 7 min
0:15 build 2

In Global Workflows

Create the trigger workflow.

A new workflow with a survey-response trigger. Pick the survey from Live 1. Name it clearly — naming conventions matter at scale. Something like "200-Level-WB-SFDC-CSAT-Response."

⏱ 10 min
0:25 build 3

Workflow step · 1 of 2

Add the decision step.

One condition: only write back if a specific question (CSAT score, for example) is below a threshold. You pick the threshold — picking it is the learning. Without the gate, every response writes back and floods the record with noise.

⏱ 15 min
0:40 build 4

Workflow step · 2 of 2 · The key deliverable

Add the Salesforce update task.

Choose "Update object." Load credentials. Pick the object (Contact, in most cases). Map the Salesforce Object ID from the survey flow into the lookup field. Pick one field to write back. Slow down here — this is where customers stall in production.

⏱ 15 min
0:55 build 5

Save the work

Save and arm the workflow.

Save it. Then confirm the workflow status reads "active" — not draft. Facilitator screen-shares the active state so the whole cohort knows what to look for.

⏱ 10 min
1:05 test

Run it

Take the survey.

Open the test email from Live 1. Complete the survey. Pick an answer that satisfies the decision condition you set in Build 3. Submit.

⏱ 7 min
1:12 verify · 1

Easy verification

Check Data and Analysis.

Confirm the response is captured and that the embedded data — including the Salesforce Object ID — is on the row. This is the easy verification. The next one is the hard one.

⏱ 6 min
1:18 verify · 2

The closing beat

Check Salesforce. Watch the field change.

Open Salesforce. Navigate to the original contact record. Confirm the field has been updated. The room watches the value change on-screen. This is the ah-ha moment for the whole lab — we'll take the silence.

⏱ 7 min
1:25 close

Path to production

From sandbox to live.

Switch credentials. Switch to your production list. Increase the schedule cadence. Monitor for the first week. Side quest: document the build in your internal runbook — Module 04 gives you the template.

⏱ 5 min

Side quest · Days 10–14

Document the build. Move it to production.

The lab outcome is a working integration. Your actual outcome is taking it to production. Module 04 gives you a runbook template with three sections — credentials and access, schedule and cadence, monitoring and alerts.

Writing the runbook forces you to confront the parts you didn't fully understand. That's the value — not the document, the writing.
