Module Two · Live webinar · Day 5

Live

Pull the data. Send the survey.

Salesforce → Qualtrics. 90 minutes. Built alongside the cohort.

Live Session 1 is the extract-and-distribute half. You'll build a workflow that pulls a Salesforce report on a schedule, maps the fields into XM Directory, and sends a transactional survey to the contacts that came across. By the end of 90 minutes, you'll have wired the first half of a real integration — and a test survey will be sitting in your own inbox.

Bring the credentials you saved in pre-work. Bring the survey you copied. Bring patience — this is the session where most customers hit their first XMD mapping decision and have to commit to a structure. We'll work through it together.

Cameras on if you can. Two screens recommended.

What the cohort leaves with

  • A scheduled ETL workflow running in your own instance, pulling from a real Salesforce report
  • A directory list with deduplication and frequency rules configured
  • A transactional survey wired to embedded data from Salesforce
  • A test email sitting in your inbox — proof that the wire works
  • A side quest: re-run the workflow, do not complete the survey, wait for Live Session 2

Before you join

Show up with the wire pre-loaded.

Live Session 1 starts on the build. There is no setup time during the session — every minute of pre-work you did pays off here. Confirm these four before Day 5.

🔑
From pre-work Salesforce credentials accessible Stored in the Qualtrics workflow credential store, or in your password manager. We'll authenticate at Build 5 — if you're hunting for credentials then, the cohort waits. Module 01 · Pre-work checklist →
📊
In Salesforce A report with at least ten contacts Test data only. Must include an Account, Case, or Client ID column — that's the unique identifier we'll map. Missing this column is the most common pre-work miss.
📝
In Qualtrics Your copy of the transactional survey The survey you copied or imported in pre-work. Published. We won't edit it in the session — Build 8 just pulls embedded data into it.
🖥️
Tech check Two screens if you have them One for the webinar, one for your build. Not required, but the session is screen-heavy — Salesforce on one side, Qualtrics on the other.

Rules of the room

Four things that make this work.

  1. 1 We build, we don't watch. Stay in your Qualtrics instance the full 90 minutes. When the facilitator builds a step, you build it too.
  2. 2 Speak up when you're stuck. Raise your hand in chat. Two people stuck on the same thing means we stop and debug it on-screen — the whole cohort learns.
  3. 3 Sandbox by default. Build in your Salesforce sandbox unless you have a clear reason not to. Production is fine but please flag it in chat so the producer can watch your build.
  4. 4 Mapping is the lab. Build 6 — the XMD mapping — is where most customers stall in production. We'll slow down there. Slowing down is the work.

The 90 minutes

What happens during the build.

Eight builds, one test, one closing side quest. Every step is a parallel build — the facilitator stays in their instance alongside you in yours.

0:00 open

Kickoff

The rule: we build, we don't watch.

Producer confirms everyone has Salesforce credentials accessible. No icebreakers — the build is the icebreaker.

⏱ 3 min
0:03 anchor

Context

The ETL frame — five minutes, one diagram.

Extract from report. Transform fields. Load into XMD. Where the pattern breaks: stale reports, mapping drift, deduplication gaps. Honest about all three.

⏱ 5 min
0:08 build 1

In Salesforce

Verify your report.

Open your sandbox. Confirm the report exists, contains only test data, and includes Account/Case/Client ID as a column.

⏱ 7 min
0:15 build 2

In Qualtrics

Create the XMD list.

A new directory list named after the lab. Enable contact frequency and deduplication rules — over-surveying is the fastest way to lose stakeholder trust.

⏱ 10 min
0:25 build 3

In Qualtrics

Open your survey.

Verify the survey you copied in pre-work is published. Five minutes max — we don't edit it here.

⏱ 5 min
0:30 build 4

In Global Workflows

Create the ETL workflow.

A new scheduled workflow. Name it. Schedule it less frequently than the Salesforce report runs — rule of thumb, daily report paired with a workflow that runs daily, one hour later.

⏱ 10 min
0:40 build 5

Workflow task · 1 of 3

Add the Salesforce extractor.

Authenticate with stored credentials. Choose "Pull from report" and pick your report. Skip the Transform step on the first pass — it's an option for later, not a requirement now.

⏱ 15 min
0:55 build 6

Workflow task · 2 of 3 · The key deliverable

Map Salesforce columns to XMD fields.

Add the XM Directory task. Decide which field is the unique identifier, which fields become embedded data, which fields get ignored. The session slows here on purpose — mapping is where customers stall in production. Get it right in lab.

⏱ 15 min
1:10 build 7

In your survey

Pull embedded data into the survey.

Use "Pull from contact list" on the survey's embedded data block. Tie embedded data fields to the XMD fields you just mapped. Save. Publish. This is when two systems start to feel like one.

⏱ 8 min
1:18 build 8

Workflow task · 3 of 3

Add the Distributions task.

Configure email distribution to the XMD list. Pick the survey. The distribution will start after the load completes.

⏱ 7 min
1:25 run it

The closing beat

Run it. Watch the inbox.

Facilitator runs the workflow manually. You run yours. Two minutes of silence — the room watches the emails arrive in their own inboxes. This is the win.

⏱ 3 min
1:28 close

Side quest + what's next

The side quest — and why you can't take your own survey yet.

Re-run your workflow once before Day 9, but do not complete the survey. Live Session 2 needs an open response to write back from.

Side quest · Before Day 9

Run it again. Don't take the survey.

Before Live Session 2, re-run your workflow once on your own — it'll send a fresh test email. Do not complete the survey. Live 2 needs the response to remain open so we can write back from it.

If you accidentally submit it, that's fine — just take it again. We'll need an unfinished response to demo against on Day 9.

🎯
Unlock condition for Live Session 2 An open survey response in your instance. The write-back demo fires off of yours.
