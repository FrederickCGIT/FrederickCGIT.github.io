L1

Lesson 1 · ~15 min read

Integration basics.

Why we integrate, the four patterns, and where ETL fits.

Experience data without operational data is half a picture. The integration is what makes the feedback loop close. You'll wire Salesforce to Qualtrics in both directions — request goes one way, response goes back the other.

There are four integration patterns you'll meet again and again: connectors, public APIs, flat files, and event-driven workflows. If you cleared L0, you've already seen these. The 200 Level adds depth on one of them — the ETL pattern — because that's what the Salesforce build runs on.

ETL in one sentence

Extract data from Salesforce. Transform the fields so they map cleanly. Load the contacts into XM Directory. That's the whole pattern. Live Session 1 builds it. Live Session 2 closes the loop the other way.

What Salesforce gives you to pull from

Three options: Contact, Account/Case, or Report. The recommendation is Report. Reports are flexible and you own them — you can change the columns without raising a ticket against your Salesforce admin. We'll use a report for the lab build.

Three transactional use cases worth knowing

  • CSAT survey triggered off case close
  • Renewal NPS triggered off contract anniversary
  • Onboarding pulse triggered off account creation

A–Z

Reference · Glossary

Eight terms, plain.

If you've cleared L0, most of these will be review. Worth a skim regardless.

XMD
XM Directory — the contact list that holds the people you'll survey. Where Salesforce contacts land after the workflow runs.
Embedded Data
Fields that travel with a respondent through a survey without being asked as questions. The Salesforce Object ID rides here.
ETL
Extract, Transform, Load. The shape every Salesforce-to-Qualtrics integration follows, whether anyone names it that way.
Workflow
A sequence of automated tasks in Qualtrics. Lives in Global Workflows. Scheduled (clock) or event-driven (trigger).
Trigger
The thing that starts a workflow. For Live 1 it's a schedule. For Live 2 it's a survey response.
Extractor
The workflow task that pulls data out of Salesforce. Specifically: the Salesforce Extractor with "Pull from report" — not the generic Report Extractor.
Task
One step inside a workflow. Extract, distribute, update, decide — each is a task. Order matters.
Object ID
Salesforce's unique identifier for a record. The single most important field in this lab — without it, the write-back has nothing to find.
