04

Module Four · Community · Days 10–14

Make it ship.

You built the integration. Now move it into production.

The lab outcome was a working integration in your sandbox. Your outcome is the integration running in production — feeding real surveys to real customers and writing real responses back. That's the metric that matters. Module 04 is what gets you there.

Three moves, all in community, ~45 minutes total. Short by design. Each one is small. Each one makes the build harder to walk away from.

What you built

Five artifacts. One closed loop.

Live in your own instance — verified, not self-reported.

  • An ETL workflow · pulling a Salesforce report on schedule
  • An XM Directory list · with deduplication and frequency rules configured
  • A transactional survey · wired to embedded data from Salesforce, including the Object ID
  • An event-driven workflow · firing on survey response with a decision step that gates write-backs
  • A verified write-back · the field changed on-screen in Salesforce. You watched it happen.

That's a real integration. Nothing about it is a demo. The only thing standing between this build and production use is the work in this module.

01

Move one · ~10 min

Post a public commit.

Four sentences in community. Naming the date makes the deadline real.

Tell the cohort who you are, what the integration is for, and when it's going live. Four sentences max — the constraint is the feature. Once it's posted, the deadline is no longer private, and that changes how the next two weeks of work get prioritized.

02

Move two · ~25 min

Write the runbook.

A short doc your future self will thank you for. Three sections.

Every production integration needs a runbook. We give you the template; you fill in what's true for your build. The value isn't the finished document — it's the act of writing it. Gaps you didn't notice during the live sessions surface here, while they're still cheap to fix.

  1. Section 01

    Credentials & access

    Which Salesforce account owns the credentials. Where they're stored. Who has access. Rotation cadence.

  2. Section 02

    Schedule & cadence

    When the ETL workflow runs. When the report it pulls from updates. Why those two cadences are aligned.

  3. Section 03

    Monitoring & alerts

    Who gets paged when the workflow fails. What "fails" means. The first thing to check when something looks off.

📂 The template · download & fill

Three sections matching the three beats above. Fillable. Roughly one page per section. Use it for this build — then keep using it for every future integration.
