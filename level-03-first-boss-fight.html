Before you join

Show up ready to build.

This isn't a demo. It's a build session. Come with the artifacts from Levels 01 and 02 and you'll be exactly where you need to be when the session starts.

You'll be working in your own Qualtrics instance the entire time. Make sure you can log in before Day 5.

Rules of the room

Four things that make this work.

  1. 1 We build, we don't watch. Stay logged into Qualtrics for the full 90 minutes. When the facilitator builds a step, you build it too.
  2. 2 Stuck points are the point. Raise your hand in chat when you can't move forward. The whole cohort learns when we debug live.
  3. 3 Bring your own context. The survey you build is a real project intake for your org — not a demo with fake data. Use your actual field names.
  4. 4 No such thing as a dumb question here. This cohort is all new customers. Nobody knows this yet. Ask early and often.

The 90 minutes

What happens during the build.

Six segments, one survey at the end. The facilitator stays in your Qualtrics instance alongside you — every step is a parallel build, not a demo.

0:00 open

Kickoff

The one rule: we build, we don't watch.

The facilitator sets the tempo. Log in, open your loadout sheet, and get ready to build. The icebreaker is the build itself.

⏱ 5 min
0:05 anchor

Context

Why a project intake survey is the right first build.

Five minutes on why this specific survey pays off every quarter. It's the architecture choice that makes Level 04's ticket flow work. Short. No slides beyond a diagram.

⏱ 5 min
0:10 build 1

First build · Your instance

Usage tags — track how your platform gets used.

Everyone creates usage tags in their own instance. The facilitator screen-shares and narrates. You follow along. Pauses every few minutes for stuck points.

📦 Output: Usage tags live in your instance
0:25 build 2

Second build · Your instance

Survey skeleton — the standard intake fields.

Open the project intake template. Build the core fields together. The facilitator explains the why behind each field, not just the what. Your loadout sheet drives the embedded data fields.

📦 Output: Intake survey skeleton live in your instance
0:55 build 3

Third build · Your instance

Display logic — branching by project type.

Add conditional logic so different project types show different follow-up questions. This is usually where the most interesting stuck points come up — two volunteers debug live with the group.

This is the moment that teaches the most. Raise your hand early.
1:15 test

Quiet build

Send the survey to yourself.

Everyone tests their own survey. Five minutes of quiet. Fill it out from the respondent's view. Check that your embedded data fields populate correctly.

📦 Output: Tested, verified intake survey
1:25 close

Handoff

Side quest + preview of Level 04.

Side quest: test the survey with one teammate before Level 04. Preview of what Level 04 builds on top of what you made today. Calendar invite for Level 04 drops in chat.

Side quest · Before Level 04

Test the survey with one teammate.

Before Level 04, send the survey to one person on your team. Ask them to submit a real — or realistic — project request. Watch the response come in. Check the embedded data fields populated.

This isn't optional busywork. Level 04 builds a ticket flow on top of this survey. If the survey has gaps, you'll find them now when they're easy to fix.
