Qualtrics Labs · 200 Level

Salesforce
Integration Lab.

In the 200 Level, Qualtrics stops being a survey tool and starts being a system. You'll wire it to Salesforce both directions — pulling contacts from a report, distributing a survey, and writing the response back to the original record.

Two live sessions, four days of pre-work, one working integration at the end. By the time you finish, you'll have a build running in your sandbox that you can take to production the next morning.

Before you enroll

Bring three things.

If any of these are missing, the lab won't ship.

The 200 Level is denser than the 100 series. Cohorts cap at 20 because stuck-points take longer to debug when two systems are talking. We'd rather you arrive ready than join unready and fall behind.

What you'll need on Day 1

  • A Qualtrics CX license that includes the Salesforce connector
  • Salesforce credentials with access to reports and export permissions (sandbox strongly preferred)
  • About four hours of pre-work time before the first live session

New to Qualtrics? Start with the L0 Start-Up Lab → Customers who clear the 200 Level are typically the ones who cleared L0 first — they already know workflows, embedded data, and have a survey to wire up.

What you'll build

Three working pieces.
One closed loop.

Experience data without operational data is half a picture. The integration is what closes the loop — and you leave with it running, not described on a slide.

  • Piece 01 · The pull

    ETL

    A scheduled workflow that pulls a Salesforce report, transforms the columns, and loads contacts into XM Directory on a cadence you control.

    Built in Module 02

  • Piece 02 · The send

    Survey

    A transactional survey wired to embedded data from Salesforce, distributed by email to the contacts the workflow just loaded.

    Built in Module 02

  • Piece 03 · The return

    Write-back

    An event-driven workflow that fires on survey response, decides what to write, and updates the matching Salesforce record using the original Object ID.

    Built in Module 03
