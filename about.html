About us

We started with
one sweater and
a bad idea.

In 2019, Nora bought a hand-spun llama-wool sweater at a roadside market outside Cusco. She wore it for nine days straight. Her sister Bea — who had a retail lease and no plan — said the words that built a company: "Can you get eleven more?"

What we actually mean by it

Three rules, and
we do keep them

Every claim below is auditable.
Ask and we'll send the paperwork.

01

Traceable to a hillside

Our fiber comes from four co-op ranches in the Peruvian highlands. We publish the ranch, the shearing season, and what we paid. Nobody asked us to. We did it anyway.

02

Small batches, on purpose

We'd rather sell out than sit on inventory. It makes the restock emails annoying and the warehouse small, which is the trade we chose.

03

Refill before you replace

Every beauty product with a pump is refillable. Bring the bottle into any boutique, get 20% off the refill, and keep the glass forever.

A necessary clarification

We do not
sell alpaca.

We get the email roughly twice a week. Alpacas are lovely. Alpacas are also a completely different animal with a completely different fiber and a completely different personality, and we have made our choice.

One of our eye balms is named after them. That is the extent of the relationship.

How we got here

Seven years, abridged

  1. 2019

    One rack in a Marfa storefront

    Twelve sweaters. Sold out in a weekend to people who were mostly there for the art.

  2. 2021

    Beauty launches by accident

    A customer asked what we used on our hands. Eighteen months later it was a product line.

  3. 2023

    Household, and the Llamp

    We made 200 lamps as a joke. They sold in four hours. The joke is now a category.

  4. 2026

    Nine boutiques, one website

    Austin, Marfa, Santa Fe, Denver, Nashville, Charleston, Portland, Brooklyn, and Chicago.
